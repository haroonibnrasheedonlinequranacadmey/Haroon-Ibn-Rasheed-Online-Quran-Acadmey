Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our academy

What does the academy teach?

We provide Quran Reading, Quran Translation, Quran Tafseer, Quran Memorization, Norani & Madni Qaida, Tajweed-o-Qirat, 40 Hadiths, Prayers & Kalmas, Tilawat Video & Voice Editing, and Islamic Studies for Kids.

Are classes online?

Yes. Classes are conducted online using Google Meet and Zoom.

How long is one class?

Each regular class is 30 minutes.

Can students choose a male or female teacher?

Yes. Male and female teachers are available according to the student's requirements.

Can a teacher be changed?

Yes. If a student is not comfortable with the assigned teacher, the teacher can be changed.

Is a trial class available?

Yes. A 3-day FREE trial is available for new students.

How can I contact the academy?

You can contact Haroon Ibn Rasheed Online Quran Academy through WhatsApp using the WhatsApp button available on this website.

About Haroon Ibn Rasheed Online Quran Academy

Haroon Ibn Rasheed Online Quran Academy is an online Quran learning academy dedicated to helping students learn the Holy Quran and Islamic studies from the comfort of their homes.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make Quran and Islamic education accessible to children and adults through convenient online classes with qualified teachers and a comfortable learning environment.

Our Courses

We offer Quran Reading, Quran Translation, Quran Tafseer, Quran Memorization, Norani & Madni Qaida, Tajweed-o-Qirat, 40 Hadiths, Prayers & Kalmas, Tilawat Video & Voice Editing, and Islamic Studies for Kids.

Online Classes

Classes are conducted online through Google Meet and Zoom. Regular classes are 30 minutes long and students can learn according to their scheduled class time.
